# Centoire draft presentation audit

Reviewed 14 September 2026 via the Canva browser editor. All 14 pages of each supplied draft were read through their accessible slide content. Representative rendered pages were visually inspected. Embedded charts can expose incomplete accessible text, so final visual/chart QA remains necessary. No slide content was edited.

Drafts:

- [Competitive Edge / Competitive Analysis](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHUzJoTSv8/gPGNhS10uom5oaFCM5wLgw/edit)
- [Investor pitch deck](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHUzJ1w4FA/SPkp0SSfgfKWTKjoy4g77A/edit)

No product presentation was supplied. References S01–S19 point to the [source register](source-register.md).

## Highest-priority corrections

1. Remove current-tense jobs, research tools, sourcing, certification, and startup/investor capabilities: the reviewed routes render Coming Soon pages.
2. Replace unsupported market sizing with a bottom-up model. The draft does not substantiate $47B TAM or 40M eligible professionals.
3. Separate implemented functionality, enabled production behavior, actual adoption, and future ambition.
4. Replace “only/no competitor” statements with a defensible, researched positioning hypothesis.
5. Reconcile $15 Pro, $30 Studio, $200 annual SAM assumptions, and $30 monthly SOM assumptions.
6. Add team and a current fundraising ask; product inventory is not traction.

## Design assessment

The pitch uses a blue gradient corporate template; the competitor cover uses an orange background with outlined uppercase display text. Neither matches the newer platform's warm editorial identity. Replace both with shared sand/charcoal/coral masters and Playfair Display/Plus Jakarta Sans typography. Preserve useful business content after correction, but rebuild hierarchy and slide density around one supported takeaway per page.

Final deck review must inspect every rendered page, including the technology and funds charts that did not fully expose their data through accessibility text.
